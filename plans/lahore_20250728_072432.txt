Day 1: Arrival and Exploring Old Lahore
- Arrive in Lahore.
- Visit the Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila) and Badshahi Mosque.
- Explore the narrow streets of Old Lahore, including the famous Gali Surjan Singh.
- Enjoy traditional Lahori cuisine for dinner.

Day 2: Historical Sites
- Visit the Minar-e-Pakistan.
- Explore the Lahore Museum.
- Stroll through the Bagh-e-Jinnah (Jinnah Garden).
- Enjoy local street food for lunch.

Day 3: Cultural Immersion
- Visit the Wagah Border for the flag-lowering ceremony.
- Explore the Anarkali Bazaar.
- Enjoy a traditional Lahori dinner.

Day 4: More Historical Sites
- Visit the Tomb of Jahangir and the Nur Jahan Tomb.
- Explore the Akbari Sarai.
- Enjoy local cuisine for lunch.

Day 5: Last Day in Lahore
- Spend your morning shopping for souvenirs or visiting any last-minute sites.
- Depart from Lahore.

Budget Breakdown (approximate):
- Accommodation (avg. 1,500 PKR per night for 5 nights): 7,500 PKR
- Food and dining: 2,000 PKR (avg. 400 PKR per day for meals and snacks)
- Transportation and miscellaneous: 500 PKR

Total: 10,000 PKR
