Day 1:
- Morning: Explore Old Lahore Food Street. Try traditional Lahori breakfast items like Nihari and Halwa Puri.
- Afternoon: Wander through the Walled City, visiting architectural marvels like Badshahi Mosque and Lahore Fort, with quick stops for street snacks.
- Evening: Dinner at Gawalmandi Food Street, sampling various kebabs, tikkas, and other BBQ delights.

Day 2:
- Morning: Visit Anarkali Bazaar for a diverse range of street food, including Golgappay, Dahi Bhallay, and Samosas.
- Afternoon: Explore the modern cafes and eateries in DHA, experiencing a different side of Lahore's food scene.
- Evening: Enjoy a traditional Lahori dinner at a renowned restaurant, focusing on dishes like Lahori Karahi and Paya.

Day 3:
- Morning: Indulge in a hearty breakfast at a famous spot for Phajje ke Paye or Siri Paye.
- Afternoon: Stroll through Liberty Market, trying local desserts like Falooda and Kulfi, and perhaps some shopping.
- Evening: Farewell dinner at a high-end restaurant, offering a fusion of traditional and contemporary Pakistani cuisine.
