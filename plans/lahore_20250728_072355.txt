Day 1: Arrival and Exploring Old Lahore
- Morning: Arrive in Lahore, check into your hotel.
- Afternoon: Visit the Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila) and Badshahi Mosque.
- Evening: Explore the streets of Old Lahore, visit the Lahore Museum, and enjoy traditional dinner at a local restaurant.

Day 2: Historical Sites
- Morning: Visit the Tomb of Jahangir and Akbari Sarai.
- Afternoon: Explore the Wazir Khan Mosque and the surrounding bazaar.
- Evening: Enjoy a traditional Lahori dinner.

Day 3: Culinary Delights
- Morning: Try traditional Lahori breakfast at a local eatery.
- Afternoon: Visit a local food street or market to sample various street foods.
- Evening: Enjoy a cooking class to learn how to make traditional Lahori dishes.

Day 4: More History and Culture
- Morning: Visit the Data Darbar shrine.
- Afternoon: Explore the Chajju Bagh area and visit the Mughal-era tombs.
- Evening: Attend a cultural performance or visit a local Sufi shrine.

Day 5: Last Day in Lahore
- Morning: Visit any last-minute historical sites or shopping.
- Afternoon: Depart for the airport or your next destination.
